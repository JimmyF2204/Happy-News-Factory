About Smiling Times

Smiling Times collects uplifting news from public RSS feeds and links to the original articles.

What is included

Stories about a concrete positive development: peace agreements, acts of kindness, medical and scientific progress, conservation successes, rescues, recoveries and hopeful human-interest stories.

What is left out

Stories mainly about violence, tragedy, disasters, ongoing wars, environmental damage, economic hardship, political outrage, crime or gossip, and articles that need payment or an account to read.

How it works

A program checks the feeds every hour and scores each headline and summary with word lists. High-scoring stories are published automatically once the article page loads in full without sign-in. Uncertain stories wait for a human editor. The region shown is the publisher's region. Summaries are short excerpts from the publisher's own feed and are not AI-generated.

Limitations

Automatic filtering makes mistakes, and this site is neither complete nor perfectly objective. Facts are not independently verified; please rely on the original publisher.
