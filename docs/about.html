About Smiling Times

Smiling Times collects good and hopeful news from public RSS feeds and links to the original articles.

What is included

Peace agreements, kindness, health and science progress, discoveries, nature and wildlife, clean energy, culture, sport, and other stories that are not bad news.

What is left out

Stories whose headline is about violence, death, disasters, war, crime or economic hardship, unless they report peace or a clearly hopeful development. Articles that are certain to need payment or an account are also left out.

How it works

A program checks the feeds every hour and screens each headline and summary with English and Swedish word lists. Articles with a clear paywall are dropped; when access is uncertain the story is still shown. A few mixed cases wait for a human editor. The region shown is the publisher's region. Summaries are short excerpts from the publisher's own feed and are not AI-generated.

Translation

Stories in other languages are machine-translated to English by MyMemory and labelled, with the original headline shown. The language menu opens the page through Google Translate. Machine translation makes mistakes; the original article is the reliable version.

Photos

Photos are the publishers' own preview images, loaded from their websites and credited to them. Publishers who want a photo or story removed can write to the address below.

Limitations

Automatic filtering makes mistakes, and this site is neither complete nor perfectly objective. Facts are not independently verified; please rely on the original publisher.
